# nexa-tel-customer-churn-analysis
An end-to-end telecom data analytics project on NexaTel using Python for ETL, DAX/SQL for modeling, and Power BI for interactive dashboards to analyze churn drivers and improve customer retention.

**📄 Executive Summary**
Project Title: Telecom Customer Churn & Retention Analysis

Business Context:

An end-to-end data analysis was conducted on a dataset comprising 24 relational tables to address customer churn and enhance revenue retention for a telecom enterprise. The project involved data cleaning and ETL using Python, data modeling via SQL/DAX, and interactive visualization in Power BI to derive actionable business insights.

Key Findings:

Overall Churn Rate: Approximately 18% to 22% of the active customer base discontinues service annually.

Network Performance Impact: High call drop rates and degraded data download speeds are the primary drivers of customer dissatisfaction and churn.

Support Resolution Bottlenecks: Customers experiencing support ticket resolution times exceeding 24 hours exhibit a significantly higher probability of churning.

Early-Stage Vulnerability: Customers within their first 6 months of tenure demonstrate the highest churn risk.

Revenue Risk Exposure: A substantial portion of churning customers belongs to the High Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) segment, directly impacting bottom-line profitability.

💡 Key Recommendations
Network Infrastructure Optimization:

Prioritize cell tower upgrades and bandwidth allocation in geographic regions flagged with low network quality scores and high drop rates.

Targeted SLA for High-Value Customers:

Implement a dedicated Service Level Agreement (SLA) for High ARPU subscribers to ensure support ticket resolution within 24 hours.

Structured Onboarding & Loyalty Incentives:

Introduce targeted retention offers and loyalty discounts for new subscribers during their first 3 to 6 months.

Automated Feedback & Retention Triggers:

Establish automated outreach protocols for customers issuing low CSAT scores or filing multiple technical support tickets within a short timeframe.
